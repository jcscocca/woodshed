# Scales and chords inside the existing lessons

The course remains **eight units and 80 twenty-minute visits**. Twenty-three
existing visits now connect preparation more directly to the music already in
the course. Three new exercises fill specific gaps; the other changes reuse
existing instruction. The former separate bridge units are removed from active
Guided Practice, Catalogue and review.

The course contains 196 exercises. Another 441 optional setups remain in the
Scales tab's **All-key reference**, making 637 active exercises overall. These
setups cover other keys, minor forms, ranges, arpeggios and motion patterns when
needed. They are not another required completion path. There are 50 active
understanding questions, 15 historical definitions and nine supported transfer
families. All 637 existing scores remain unchanged by the audit fixes.

## What the music needs

Preparation follows the written notes, register and hand movement. A full scale
does not automatically prepare a passage that uses fixed fingers or a released
chord jump. Lesson steps and visit goals now name the connection explicitly.
The existing destination scores, source disclosures and musical identities
remain unchanged.

The three added preparations are:

- **Canon bars 3–4** (`d-canon-minor-lh-prep`): LH B2 → D3/F♯3/B3, then F♯2 →
  A2/C♯3/F♯3. Each move releases the bass before travelling to the upper chord.
- **Mozart's five-note shape** (`a6-mozart-key-shape`): A4/B4/C♯5/D5/E5 on
  fingers 1–5. The opening C♯–D–C♯–E gesture uses 3–4–3–5 without a scale crossing.
- **Switchyard's sevenths** (`e7-blues-sevenths`): E7, A7 and B7 include their
  actual seventh tones. E7's G♯ does not cancel the melody's intentional G
  naturals; the piece keeps its written blues colours, rhythm and bass fifths.

The standalone A-major RH octave worksheet remains optional reference. Scheduled
LH/both-hands scale work and its RH crossing instructions remain available in
the course. Mozart receives the narrower shape its opening actually uses.

## Saved progress and revised plans

Changed visits receive a new `music-v2` plan identity while retaining their
original unit/day/slot. The other 57 visits keep their original identities.
Original plans remain available to interpret saved work:

- Saved blocks, guided-block practice, a saved session review or an unfinished
  lesson/review draft normally retain that visit's original plan. Resume and
  Revisit therefore use the blocks the learner actually started or completed.
- An untouched changed visit uses the revised plan. Old completion marks never
  become credit for a different preparation or block position.
- **Preview updated preparation** opens the revision separately. If the revised
  plan is completed while the original is unfinished, the completed revision is
  displayed and the visit counts once toward the course. **Resume earlier
  Day … · Session …** still opens the untouched earlier plan. A completed
  original remains complete when the revision is only partial; the app never
  combines partial versions to claim completion.
- Reports, minutes, notes, assessments, first-return evidence and backup data
  remain attached to their original identities. No new scope or mastery evidence
  is invented during migration.
- An unfinished review or fresh-example draft restores its exact source lesson,
  variant and score, even when the due queue has changed or become empty. Its
  initial before-playback report remains separate from subsequent repair.
- Removed-unit reports retain readable historical titles. A previously selected
  removed unit opens an appropriate existing unit. Historical question answers
  remain readable through 15 retained definitions; these questions and removed
  studies are not offered as new active work.

Draft restoration remains limited to the same browser tab. Durable saved history
continues to support normal export/import; drafts and audio takes are not backup
history. See [README data guidance](../README.md#data-installation-and-compatibility).

## Evidence and review limits

The **prior integration run**, before the critical audit fixes, passed local
`npm test` and the production build. Across its full browser run and targeted
rerun, all 37 applicable scenarios passed; two duplicate PWA scenarios were
intentionally skipped. That run does not certify the later storage, scheduling
and interface changes. Final post-audit verification is recorded in
[release readiness](release-readiness.md). A live in-app-browser check inspected
the unit list, historical reports, Canon preparation and Mozart's five-note
shape, plus the E7/A7/B7 preparation.
[Audit fixes](audit-fixes.md) explains the subsequent changes and boundaries.
The post-audit Node suites, including the SongEditor reset guard, pass on Node 22
and 26, and both final builds pass. The clean production-browser run passes 58
of 60 scheduled cases, with two intentional PWA project skips. Its IAB walkthrough
confirmed the corrected C4B goal, exact LH-bar-4/tempo-48/note restoration and owned
playback controls. Release readiness records that scope separately from the
pending human and hosted-CI gates.

An independent teacher should still check physical comfort, preparation quality
and pacing, followed by the learner pilot. The changes make the teaching intent
explicit; passing software tests does not establish learning effectiveness.
